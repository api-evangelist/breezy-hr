# Breezy HR
> Improve your entire hiring process with Breezy HR. Our applicant tracking system (ATS) helps you attract & hire quality employees in less time. 

## Products - [Breezy HR ATS](https://breezy.hr/): End-to-end applicant tracking system that automates job posting to 50+ boards, streamlines candidate screening with drag-drop pipelines, and simplifies hiring with built-in offers and compliance tools 
- [Breezy Intelligence](https://breezy.hr/p/breezy-intelligence): AI-powered hiring features that detect and block fake resumes, automatically score candidate fit, and generate interaction summaries to speed up decisions 
- [Breezy Onboard](https://breezy.hr/onboard): Automated onboarding platform that guides new hires from offer acceptance to day one with role-based workflows and paperless document management 
- [Breezy Perform](https://breezy.hr/perform): Performance management software for running review cycles, setting cascading goals, conducting 1:1s, and giving continuous feedback
